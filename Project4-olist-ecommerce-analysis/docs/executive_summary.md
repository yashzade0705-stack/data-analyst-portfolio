# Executive summary - Olist marketplace (Jan 2017 - Aug 2018)

**Situation.** Monthly revenue grew from R$ 111,798 to R$ 838,577 (+650%) over the period (R$ 13.18M in total), but only 3.0% of customers ever bought again.

**What we found**
1. Revenue is fairly diversified: the top category (health_beauty) is 9.3% of revenue and the top 10 sellers account for 13.3%.
2. Late deliveries (6.77% of delivered orders) average 2.27 stars vs 4.29 for on-time orders, a significant gap with a large effect size (p < 0.001, r = -0.64). Even 1-3 days late averages 3.29 stars; 8+ days late averages 1.70. This is an association rather than proven cause.
3. Delivery times vary from 8.8 days (SP) to 29.4 days (RR).
4. Only 2,801 of 93,358 customers (3.0%) became repeat customers.

**Recommendations**
1. Second-order / win-back campaign. *Example success metric: repeat rate from 3.0% to 4.0%.*
2. Improve delivery-date accuracy and prioritise the slowest states. *Example metric: Late Delivery % below 5%.*
3. Monitor seller delivery performance, since seller concentration is moderate. *Example metric: top-10 seller share stays near 13%.*

**Caveats.** Observational data; no margin or cost data; sparse months outside Jan 2017 - Aug 2018 excluded; review scores are self-selected.

## Project 1: Sales Performance Dashboard
![Sales dashboard](Project1-sales-dashboard/Images/dashboard.png)

**Goal:** show how sales and profit perform across regions, months and products. **Data:** about 2.30M in sales.

- Profit margin is 12.47% overall, from 7.92% in the Central region to 14.94% in the West
- September to December bring in about half of the year's sales
- One product, a Canon copier, sells more than twice as much as the next product in the top 10

**Tools:** Power BI, SQL, Excel | [Read the full project](Project1-sales-dashboard)

## Project 2: Customer Churn Analysis
![Churn dashboard](Project2-churn-analysis/images/dashboard.png)

**Goal:** find who leaves a telecom company and flag customers at risk. **Data:** about 7,000 customers.

- Churn rate is 26.58%, and about 2,400 customers are marked High risk
- Month-to-month customers churn at 42.71%, against 2.85% on two-year contracts
- Churn is 47.68% in a customer's first 12 months and falls to 9.51% after four years
- A prediction model scores every customer into a risk band

**Tools:** Python (scikit-learn), SQL, Power BI | [Read the full project](Project2-churn-analysis)

## Project 3: E-commerce Funnel Analysis
![Funnel dashboard](Project3-E-commerce-funnel-analysis/images/dashboard.png)

**Goal:** find where shoppers drop off between viewing a product and buying it. **Data:** Retailrocket events, 1.4 million viewers.

- Only about 2.5% of viewers add a product to the cart, so the biggest loss is before the cart
- About 30% of cart visitors go on to buy
- Overall, 0.73% of viewers buy
- About two thirds of purchases in the time chart happen within the first hour

**Tools:** SQLite, SQL, Python, Power BI | [Read the full project](Project3-E-commerce-funnel-analysis)

## Project 5: Food Delivery Operations Analysis
![Food delivery dashboard](Project5-food-delivery-analysis/images/dashboard_overview.png)

**Goal:** find what causes late food deliveries and how to reduce them. **Data:** about 45,000 orders.

- Late orders rise from about 15% to nearly 100% when a rider carries two or more orders at once
- Jam traffic adds about 10 minutes to the average delivery
- Fog and cloudy weather have the highest late rates, at about 38%
- Deliveries slow down from about 10 AM and peak near 8 PM

**Tools:** Python, Power BI, DAX | [Read the full project](Project5-food-delivery-analysis)
